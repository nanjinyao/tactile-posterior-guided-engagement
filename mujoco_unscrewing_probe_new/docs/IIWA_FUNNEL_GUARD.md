# KUKA iiwa Post-Capture Funnel Guard

## Scope

This module starts only after the sensor-only downward-progress/capture handoff.
It does not alter the conical fingerprint, CNN, posterior transport, outward
spiral, or their comparison baselines.

The intended hardware stack is:

```text
KUKA iiwa native Cartesian impedance at calibrated flange/TCP frame
-> external F/T sensor as observer and safety input
-> rigid adapter
-> Allen peg
-> peg-tip virtual compliance center
```

The current checkout does not contain measured flange-to-sensor or
sensor-to-tip transforms. Those transforms, gravity compensation, wrench bias,
and delay must be calibrated before hardware use.

## Physical argument

For an M8 peg with 0.10 mm single-side clearance and a 4 mm straight socket
depth, the approximate tilt that can be tolerated deep in the socket is

$$
\alpha_{deep}\lesssim\tan^{-1}\!\left(\frac{0.10}{4.0}\right)=1.43^\circ.
$$

Therefore the 15 degree conical probe must never continue after inner-wall
capture. The execution mechanism is instead upright insertion with bounded
passive angular accommodation. The simulation guard uses 0.75 degree as a
conservative initial tilt envelope; it is a commissioning value, not a
hardware-certified limit.

The impedance proxy is expressed at the peg tip $P$:

$$
\mathbf w_c^P =
\mathbf K_P(\mathbf x_d^P-\mathbf x^P)
+\mathbf D_P(\dot{\mathbf x}_d^P-\dot{\mathbf x}^P).
$$

Placing the compliance center near the contact point reduces the parasitic
moment generated by a lateral contact force. This is the virtual-RCC part of
the design. It is not a claim that the KUKA controller itself has been modified
or that a mechanical RCC has been built.

The transfer from `reference/漏斗效应.pdf` is narrow: compliant contact and
environmental constraints can passively shrink the execution-state set. That
paper uses deliberate inclined corner contact and excludes chamfers in its
experiments; it does not prove that the present M8 chamfer/socket has the same
funnel basin.

## Energy and progress shield

Let $\mathbf w_e$ be the environment-on-tool wrench at the calibrated tip and
$\Delta\mathbf x$ the measured tip twist increment. Positive robot-injected
contact work is estimated by

$$
\Delta E_{in}=\max\left(0,-\mathbf w_e^\top\Delta\mathbf x\right).
$$

Over a sliding window, downward progress and injected work are compared. A
recovery is requested only when work accumulates without sufficient depth
progress. The diagnostic health score is

$$
h=
\frac{\Delta z_{down}}{\delta_z}
-0.10\frac{\|F_{xy}\|}{|F_z|+F_0}
-0.05\frac{\|\tau_{xy}\|}{\ell(|F_z|+F_0)}.
$$

The score is not a learned classifier and is not itself a passivity proof. The
actual independent gates are:

| Gate | Meaning | Action |
|---|---|---|
| sustained contact-power limit | transient interaction is too aggressive | unload, then recover or abort |
| injected energy without depth progress | likely stall/wedge | one bounded unload/retract/upright/retouch |
| sustained tilt envelope violation | deep angular accommodation is exhausted | unload and recover |
| CAD bottom envelope crossed | commanded motion is outside known geometry | unload and safe abort; never retouch below bottom |
| first entry into CAD bottom tolerance band | possible terminal state | freeze axial command; accept expected-sign axial-force dwell or stable low-axial-wrench depth dwell; both also bound lateral force/tilt torque |

The CAD band is treated as a terminal set. Continuing the nominal descent while
waiting for a dwell counter can pass through a physically correct state and
create an artificial overshoot; freezing the command on first entry avoids
that controller error.

## Bounded recovery

Only an ordinary stall/tilt/power event is recoverable:

```text
low-force unload
-> retract 0.35 mm
-> command upright
-> rebase admittance at measured tip
-> low-force retouch
-> resume once
```

The recovery count is limited to one. A CAD-depth violation is terminal and is
reported separately as a safe abort. Hard wrench limits remain independent and
cannot be overridden by the guard.

## Simulation-initial parameters

| Quantity | Initial value |
|---|---:|
| tip planar stiffness / damping | 1.5 kN/m / 100 N s/m |
| shallow-to-deep tilt stiffness | 1.8 to 3.0 N m/rad |
| shallow-to-deep tilt damping | 0.35 to 0.50 N m s/rad |
| tilt envelope | 0.75 deg for 20 ms |
| energy/progress window | 150 ms |
| no-progress threshold | 0.02 mm |
| no-progress injected energy | 1.5 mJ |
| CAD overshoot margin | 0.25 mm |

These numbers are deliberately stored in `FunnelGuardConfig`. The architecture
can transfer to M10/M12, but the numbers cannot: clearance, depth, tip length,
adapter compliance, iiwa payload, F/T frame, friction, and controller rate all
change the admissible tilt and energy scales.

## Evidence boundary

The paired MuJoCo ablation keeps the search path, initial state, force, contact
nuisance, and post-capture TCP bias identical. It can test non-interference,
CAD-depth defense, and whether the proxy recovery remains bounded. It cannot
prove hardware passivity, real wear reduction, a larger capture basin, or
cross-size parameter transfer.

Hardware commissioning should proceed at low force in this order: calibrate
frames/bias/gravity, run the guard in shadow mode, replay only terminal-set
holds, then enable bounded unload, and enable physical retouch last.

## Extreme residual-pose stress demo

The active paired video freezes an intentionally severe post-capture fault to
make the mechanism visible. Both branches receive the same 8 degree commanded
tilt, 0.6 mm lateral TCP bias, 5 N target down-force, friction and initial
state. These disturbances are deliberately outside the nominal M8 deep-socket
geometry: the clearance/depth scale above is only 1.43 degrees.

With the current bumpless handoff controller, this disturbance is no longer a
successful-recovery example. The unsupervised branch reaches 9.71 degrees and
returns `bottom_not_verified`; on the explicitly labelled inactive display
step that follows, the raw wrench reaches 28.16 N / 131.95 mN m. The primary
terminal result remains distinct from that late display-step violation.

The tip-RCC plus supervisor branch detects a CAD-depth overshoot before the
recoverable restart sequence can own the motion. It performs a bounded unload
and returns the named `funnel_safe_abort`, with no primary or post-event hard
stop and raw peaks of 12.62 N / 31.21 mN m. Its recovery counter remains zero:
this is an unrecoverable-event rejection, not a completed
unload/retract/upright/retouch recovery.

The demo therefore supports only a simulated failure-consequence claim for one
selected severe fault. The separate fixed 12-case R0/R3 ablation carries the
completion-rate evidence. This clip is not nominal operating evidence, a
measured iiwa disturbance model, a hardware success-rate claim, or proof that
every tilted contact is recoverable.

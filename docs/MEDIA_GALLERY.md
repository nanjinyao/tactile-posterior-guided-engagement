Every distinct embedded video in the six reviewed project presentations is covered: 39 embedded references resolve to 16 unique videos by SHA-256. No animated GIF source was found. The two compact/review decks contain no embedded videos or animated GIFs; the full research deck contains 15 videos, and the earlier group-meeting deck adds one distinct short probing clip. The GIFs are viewing previews; their speed labels refer to source playback. MP4s retain the source timeline and playback rate, with audio and embedded metadata removed. Hardware viewing copies use H.264. The original no-time-limit helix clip is itself labelled accelerated in the presentation. These media add no new trials or aggregate performance claims.
